About Meridian Analytica

Meridian Analytica is an investment intelligence platform built to support equitable investment in Africa. We aggregate geospatial data, economic indicators, and governance metrics across all 54 African nations and 600+ subnational regions - delivering personalized, actionable insights to investors, advisors, and institutions operating on the continent.
